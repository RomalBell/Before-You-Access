Before You Access

A lightweight frontline decision assistant for quickly choosing the right customer-resolution path during a live interaction.
Independent portfolio concept
Product principle: Reach a clear resolution path in 1–3 questions and roughly 30–45 seconds. Use self-service when it works, Retail when it is the better path, and route known exceptions to the right channel.

    Validation evidence
    See the five scenarios that shaped the MVP

    These scenarios were used to redesign the decision model and were kept separate from the original 19-interaction discovery dataset.

    ✓ addressed
    Damaged device / claim

    Damage alone no longer forces account access; authentication and a workable assisted path are evaluated first.

    ✓ addressed
    Device settings help

    Technical complexity does not imply account access when device/manufacturer guidance can resolve the need.

    ✓ addressed
    Unexpected bill / cancel

    Account access can support diagnosis even when Retail must hand off the final action to an authorized channel.

    ✓ addressed
    Replacement-device eSIM

    A theoretical self-service route can be rejected when authentication is blocked and the workaround creates excessive friction.

    ✓ addressed
    Protection fulfillment

    Account validation is not a separate route. If verification creates the interaction and Retail can complete the need, finish the resolution in Retail.
