Key finding: No Complete Points does not automatically mean account access was unnecessary. Fulfillment, authentication, returns, payment method, device condition, and employee-only actions can change the correct route.

The prototype therefore routes by customer intent + blocker/trigger, rather than judging an interaction by sales outcome alone.
